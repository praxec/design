THE DISPATCH

A broken grid, a serif voice, and columns that mean it

This is an editorial layout built on an asymmetric two-column split with a hard rule between the lead and the sidebar. Nothing here is centered, nothing is rounded, and the type carries the personality. The reader is meant to feel the print lineage in every measure and every hairline.
